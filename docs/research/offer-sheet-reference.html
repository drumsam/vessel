How this was priced

  1. Identity first. Each line of your list is matched to one specific wine — producer, cuvée, vintage and bottle size. Where a name could mean two different wines, we stop and ask rather than guess.
  2. Live merchant offers. We read the current In Bond lists of UK merchants and keep only offers you could actually buy today: In Bond, in sterling, in stock, same wine, same vintage, same bottle size.
  3. Lowest comparable offer sets the market. Not an average, not an auction estimate. Where two or more merchants hold the wine we take the second-lowest as the reference, because a single price is not a market.
  4. Buyer discount, then our fee. Trade buyers move parcels at 20% below market. We take 8% of the sale, and the balance is yours.

Terms

Validity

Prices move with the market. This offer holds for seven days from the date above, after which the lines are re-priced against current merchant offers.

Condition

Priced on the basis of professional storage throughout and bottles in sound condition, labels and levels as expected for age. Anything found otherwise is re-quoted, not quietly discounted.

What you get

One payment on collection, per line, with the market evidence for every figure available on request — the merchant, the price and the date we read it.

Lines we cannot price

Where no UK merchant holds the wine, we say so rather than inventing a number. Those lines can go to auction or stay with you; either way you see the reasoning.
